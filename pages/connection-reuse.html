01 · Challenge

If we already have your credentials, why are we asking again?

If you've already connected your brokerage to Credit Karma, Intuit has your credentials. So why does TurboTax ask you to set up the same connection from scratch at tax time? That friction was real — and it was costing TurboTax completions.

Connection Reuse is IDX's answer: let CK members carry existing financial connections into TurboTax with a single consent. Cross-product, touching CK, TT, and Identity — each with different patterns, consent requirements, and technical constraints. Customers shouldn't feel any of that.

02 · My Tasks

Carousel reuse, bulk multi-select, back-to-back auth

I owned the IDX widget design end-to-end — the carousel reuse pattern for single-provider selection and the bulk multi-select pattern for customers with multiple eligible accounts. I built prototypes for both QBO and TurboTax contexts, iterated through multiple rounds of cross-functional feedback, and designed the back-to-back auth flow for providers requiring re-authentication on reuse.

I worked alongside DataX XD and PM on consent patterns and error states, and turned around spec updates quickly when integration requirements shifted mid-flight.

03 · Impact

Standard IDX reuse UI, adopted beyond the original launch

Launched February 2025 across CK, TT, IDX, DataX, and Identity. Eligible CK members can carry their financial connections into TurboTax with bulk import — removing one of the most friction-heavy moments in TT onboarding.

The carousel reuse and bulk multi-select patterns I designed are now the standard IDX reuse UI, adopted across multiple integrations beyond the original CK×TT launch.
