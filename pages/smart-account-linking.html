01 · Challenge

The system didn't recognize what it already knew

When a customer connects a bank account and then connects it again — different product, expired credential, long gap — the system treats it as new. Duplicate connections, fragmented data, and a "connect your account" prompt for something the customer was sure they'd already done.

Smart Account Linking detects when an incoming connection matches an existing one and resolves it intelligently — surfacing the match for confirmation, or deduplicating quietly in the background depending on context.

02 · My Tasks

Research → detection logic → resolution flows → system patterns

I led design from research through production specs. I submitted the study, led recruitment, and ran customer interviews with accountants and small business owners to understand how duplicate confusion actually felt — what made a "we found this account" prompt trustworthy, and what made it feel broken.

I designed detection and resolution flows for QBO and TTFS, worked with engineering to define triggering logic (smart linking prompt vs. silent deduplication), and delivered widget patterns now in the IDX design system.

03 · Impact

Shipped patterns + research that shaped the roadmap

Smart Account Linking shipped across QuickBooks and TurboTax. The patterns I designed are available to teams building new connection flows without solving this from scratch.

The research I led fed new requirements directly into the team's broader connection management roadmap — the kind of upstream impact that doesn't show up in a metric but shapes what gets built next.
